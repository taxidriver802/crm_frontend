# Experiment CA — expansion

Branch: `experiment/CA_expansion`

The decision log is [`../crm_backend/docs/EXPERIMENT.md`](../crm_backend/docs/EXPERIMENT.md).

This repo shows the contract on the job. The Money section states the accepted price, what has been collected, what is still owed, and what is not yet on an invoice. A deposit or an invoice payment is recorded there. Change orders are created from that section and edited as estimates. A credit line is allowed only on a change order. An approved estimate's price is locked.

Invoices show Partial, and the open balance once any money is recorded. The invoice page lists payments and can record another one. The public estimate labels a change order as a change order. The customer portal shows the agreed price, what has been paid, and what is still owed.
