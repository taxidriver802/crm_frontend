# CRM UX checklist — source: docs/research/crm-ux-expectations.md (2026-11-13). Tags: Universal / Common / Split (our pick) / Disliked (avoid) / Owner (owner's decision, 2026-11-13; outranks the research).
- Shell: at ≥lg, a fixed left sidebar 248px (`--rail-w`) holds primary nav; no top-bar primary menu; the sidebar keeps the charcoal `--chrome` rail. [Common; width and colour Owner]
- Shell: collapsed sidebar is a 60px (`--rail-w-collapsed`) icon rail, not 0px; label tooltip on hover and focus; state persists per user. [Owner]
- Shell: nav order Home, Jobs, Leads, Calendar, Tasks, Estimates, Invoices, Reports ("Leads" stays the customer list; Contacts is planned separately in contacts-plan.md); Settings and account menu pinned bottom-left; notifications bell at top-right of the header, with no second entry in the sidebar. [Common; bell Split]
- Shell: "+ New" primary button at the top of the sidebar (Job, Contact, Task, Estimate, Appointment); in the rail it shows as a "+" icon. [Split]
- Shell: global search sits at the top of the shell and matches name, street address and phone; recent items show on focus; ⌘K and "/" open it as a power-user extra, and nothing is reachable only through ⌘K. [Universal; ⌘K Owner]
- Mobile (<md): bottom tab bar of 4–5 items (Today, Jobs, Search, + with Photo first, More); no hamburger for primary nav. [Common]
- Tables: list, table and board pages fill the shared `.page-wrap` (max 1480px); never `max-w-6xl`. [Owner]
- Tables: row height 40px (`h-10`, 14px cell text, not today's 13.5px); a per-user Compact toggle gives 32px (`h-8`, 13px); 48px (`h-12`) under `(pointer: coarse)`; header row matches row height and is `sticky top-0`. [Common; toggle and 14px Owner]
- Tables: at most 6 default columns: name, address, status chip, rep avatar, next appointment or last activity, value. [Common]
- Tables: checkbox column with select-all; any selection shows a bulk bar reading "N selected"; bulk changes that fire automations ask first. [Universal]
- Tables: system views as tabs above the table (My leads / Active jobs / Needs follow-up); filters apply without saving a view; empty state is one sentence plus one primary create button. [Universal]
- Tables: numbered pagination with total ("1–50 of 312"), page size 25/50/100; no infinite scroll. [Split]
- Tables: the primary cell is a real `<a>` to the record (no `onClick` on `<tr>`); a kebab button (`h-8 w-8`) is always visible at row end; hover icons also show on `focus-within` and under `(hover: none)`. [Common; hover-only Disliked]
- Record: header shows name, address, stage chip, owner avatar, at most 2 primary buttons, then an overflow menu. [Universal]
- Record: on wide screens the key-fields panel sits on the RIGHT of the main column (the existing `.record-rail`) and stays visible; on mobile it stacks into one scroll under a sticky header. [Owner]
- Record: main column shows "Up next" (open tasks and appointments) above a newest-first history with type filters. [Universal]
- Record: notes, timeline and key fields are never behind a tab; tabs only for Photos/Files, Estimates/Invoices, Change log. [Split]
- Record and settings pages use the same `.page-wrap` (max 1480px), centred; the key-fields rail stays 328px (`20.5rem`). [Owner]
- Jobs: insurance jobs show one extra field group (carrier, claim #, adjuster, deductible); retail jobs never show it; no supplement/permit trackers. [Owner]
- Board: two boards. Leads = sales (new → sold, Lost exit); Jobs = production (Sold → in production → completed → paid), with a collapsed Pre-sale column first that holds jobs still in a selling stage (shows its count, expands on click). Closed cards are hidden by default and reachable by filter. [Owner]
- Board: stages are columns with drag between them, plus a Board / List / Map toggle on the same route and filters. [Universal; Map Owner]
- Board: drag is gated: Sold needs a signed estimate (the card returns with a message saying why); a move that messages a homeowner confirms first; every other move applies at once with Undo. [Owner]
- Board: column header shows record count and a plain $ sum; no weighted totals. [Common]
- Board: card shows name or address, value, rep avatar, next task (red text if overdue), and a days-in-stage badge that turns red past the stage threshold; card body is never tinted. [Common]
- Forms: contact, lead and task quick-create opens in a modal or right drawer (name, phone, email, address autocomplete, rep) with "Create and add another". [Split]
- Forms: job and estimate creation is a full page with one column at `max-w-2xl` (672px), sticky Save/Cancel, and a leave warning when changes are unsaved. [Split]
- Forms: required fields carry a red asterisk after the label; validate on blur, re-validate live after an error, and validate again on submit; never on the first keystroke. [Universal]
- Forms: create warns (does not block) on matching phone, email or normalized address, with an "Open existing" link. [Common]
- Editing: status, owner, dates and tags save inline on Enter or blur (Esc cancels), showing "Saved" plus an Undo toast for 5–10s; address, money and any field that messages a homeowner or fires an automation edit with explicit Save/Cancel; a failed save shows an inline error. [Owner]
- Feel: skeletons for page and list loads; a spinner inside the button for submits; a progress bar for photo uploads and any wait over 10s; no loader under 1s. [Split]
- Feel: reversible actions (archive, stage move, delete note) apply at once with an Undo toast; a confirm dialog only for irreversible actions or anything sent to a homeowner. [Common; confirm-everything Disliked]
- Visual: body text 14px/20px (`text-sm`); dense cells 13px (`text-[13px]`); captions and badges 12px (`text-xs`); no font weight below 400. [Common]
- Visual: radius 6px buttons and inputs (`rounded-md`), 8px cards and dropdowns (`rounded-lg`), 12px modals and board columns (`rounded-xl`), 4px or full for badges, full for avatars. [Common]
- Visual: surfaces separate by 1px `border-base`; resting cards use `shadow-sm` at most; `shadow-md` only on dropdowns, popovers and modals. [Common]
- Visual: status colours: green = won/paid/signed, amber = due soon/pending, red = overdue/failed/stale, blue = new/scheduled/info (a dedicated `--info` on every palette, never the accent), gray = draft/lost/archived; always with a text label; brand accent only on primary buttons, active nav, links and focus rings. [Universal; blue info Owner]
- Visual: outline icons, 16px (`size-4`) in tables and 20px (`size-5`) in nav; people get initials avatars at 24–32px `rounded-full`. [Split]
- Mobile: tap targets at least 44px on coarse pointers; job detail has tap-to-call, text and navigate buttons; photo uploads run in the background with progress and retry; no offline mode this pass. [Universal; offline Owner]
- Dark mode: per-user Light/Dark/System; every signed-in screen is covered (no white flashes); `/public/*` stays light. [Owner]
- Homeowner token pages: one per-job portal link, light and mobile-first, with company logo and rep name, estimates (clear total, Good/Better/Best cards, signature and date, confirm before approving), invoices (pay/deposit), PDF download, "Request changes", photos (each can be hidden by staff), schedule and messages. [Universal; full portal Owner]
