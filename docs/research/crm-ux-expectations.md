# Make the roofing CRM feel familiar fast

Research date: 2026-11-13. Built from eight research notes in `research_notes/CRM UX expectations roofing/`. Scope: what owners, admins and field agents (salespeople, crew leads) expect a small residential roofing/exterior CRM to look and feel like, plus what homeowners expect from token-link pages. The app uses Next 16 and Tailwind 3.4. Nearest competitors are JobNimbus, AccuLynx, Roofr, Jobber and Housecall Pro, with ServiceTitan as the heavy reference. Strength tags: **Universal** (nearly everyone does it and users notice if it is missing), **Common** (most do, with notable exceptions), **Split** (competing patterns), **Disliked** (common, but users complain). Evidence caveats are listed at the top of section 6.

## 1. Summary

- **Use a fixed left sidebar of about 240px (`w-60`) on desktop.** HubSpot (2024), Zoho (2025) and Housecall Pro (2026) all moved from top nav to a left sidebar, and none moved the other way ([HCP 2026](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/14742078-navigating-housecall-pro-new-navigation-experience); [Zoho](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/using-crm-for-everyone/transition-guide/articles/transition-guide-moving-to-zoho-crm-for-everyone-next-gen-ui)). JobNimbus still uses a top menu, and a reviewer calls its web UI due for a refresh ([Connecteam](https://connecteam.com/reviews/jobnimbus/), single source). Design systems put expanded nav at 240–256px ([Polaris](https://unpkg.com/@shopify/polaris@13/build/esm/styles.css); [Carbon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/carbon-design-system/carbon/main/packages/styles/scss/components/ui-shell/side-nav/_side-nav.scss)).
- **Collapse the sidebar to a ~64px icon rail with hover labels instead of hiding it.** Housecall Pro, Zoho, Attio and folk collapse to icons, and HubSpot users ask for the same option ([HCP](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/14742078-navigating-housecall-pro-new-navigation-experience); [HubSpot Ideas](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/HubSpot-Ideas/Left-Navigation-Menu-Show-Icons-Only/idc-p/983363/highlight/true), single source). Our sidebar currently collapses to 0px.
- **Put the Job, not the Contact, at the centre, and make the Kanban job board the signature view.** Contact and Job are separate objects. Stages run roughly Lead → Estimating → Sold → Production → AR → Completed, with Lost as an exit from any stage ([JobNimbus workflows](https://support.jobnimbus.com/what-are-best-practices-for-workflows); [Roofr job board](https://help.roofr.com/en/articles/15533109-how-to-use-the-roofr-job-board)). Every board needs a board/list toggle.
- **Search by street address and phone, not only by name.** A global search box at the top of the shell is Universal ([Salesforce](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/lightning-experience-for-salesforce-classic-users/get-your-bearings); [HCP](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/14742078-navigating-housecall-pro-new-navigation-experience)). ⌘K is Common in modern sales CRMs ([Attio](https://attio.com/help/reference/workspace/quick-search)) but absent in roofing tools, so keep it as a bonus for office staff.
- **Give each role its own home page.** Owner and admin get KPI tiles with comparison to a prior period, an "open items" block, today's schedule and an activity feed. Field agents get my day, my tasks and a quick photo/note action. This follows Housecall Pro and Jobber ([HCP homepage](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/6974306-homepage-overview-and-faq); [Jobber Home](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/23846836592023)). Keep each home to 3–5 widgets, because widget sprawl draws complaints ([Connecteam](https://connecteam.com/reviews/jobnimbus/)).
- **Run tables full width with 40px rows, a 32px compact option and 48px rows on touch.** These are Carbon's md/sm/lg sizes ([Carbon](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/data-table/style/)). Today every page sits inside `max-w-6xl`.
- **Record page: header strip, key fields kept visible beside the timeline, "Up next" above a newest-first history.** Do not hide notes, emails or key fields behind tabs ([Pipedrive deal view](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/deal-detail-view); [HubSpot 2026 layout](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/understand-the-default-record-layout)). Whether the fields sit left or right is an open question.
- **Hybrid save model.** A single-field inline edit saves on blur or Enter and shows "Saved" plus an Undo toast. Creates and multi-field edits use an explicit Save. HubSpot's silent autosave produced "did it save?" complaints ([HubSpot KB](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/update-a-property-value-for-a-record); [HubSpot Ideas](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/HubSpot-Ideas/Create-a-setting-to-turn-off-autosave-feature/idi-p/1134940)).
- **Prefer an Undo toast to a confirmation dialog.** Confirm only actions that cannot be undone, and anything sent to a homeowner, because a token link cannot be unsent ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/user-mistakes/)).
- **Keep the look neutral with one accent, but keep colour on status.** HubSpot's 2026 grayscale theme, which removed stage colours, was called "soulless" ([HubSpot Community](https://community.hubspot.com/t/hubspots-new-design-brings-your-work-into-focus/151517?page=2)). Our Ember palette maps "info" to the orange accent. That orange sits too close to warning and danger, the same red-versus-orange confusion HubSpot users reported. Add a blue info role.
- **Visual defaults:** 14px/20px body text (`text-sm`), 6px radius on controls, 8px on cards, 12px on modals, 1px borders, shadows only on floating layers ([Atlassian radius](https://atlassian.design/foundations/radius); [Carbon](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/data-table/style/)). Our radii are 2px larger at each step.
- **The phone matters more than desktop polish.** In trades tools the biggest complaints are weak mobile apps, slow or failed photo uploads, and no offline mode ([AccuLynx Field ratings](https://splitmetrics.com/apps/acculynx-field/id1485611392); [Capterra Jobber](https://www.capterra.com/p/127994/Jobber/reviews/), via snippets). Use a 4–5-item bottom tab bar, tap targets of 44px or more, and a background photo queue with retry.
- **Homeowner token pages should be light, mobile-first and branded with the company.** They need a clear total, Good/Better/Best option cards, a signature, a pay or deposit button, a PDF copy and a "request changes" path ([Jobber Client Hub](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012715008); [AccuLynx portal](https://acculynx.com/acculynx-customer-portal-for-roofing-contractors/); [Roofr proposals](https://roofr.com/proposals)). We already force light mode and the company palette there.
- **Ship sensible defaults and keep configuration light.** Contractors call AccuLynx, JobNimbus and ServiceTitan heavy for small shops, and praise Roofr and Jobber for speed ([G2 ServiceTitan](https://www.g2.com/products/servicetitan/); [ContractorToolStack](https://contractortoolstack.com/alternatives/acculynx-alternatives/)).
- **Do not churn screens people have learned.** Redesigns of scheduling and calendar screens drew complaints at Housecall Pro and AccuLynx ([Trustpilot HCP](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/housecallpro.com?page=2); [Capterra AccuLynx](https://www.capterra.com/p/116187/Acculynx/reviews/?page=2)).

## 5. Open questions

These depend on our specific users and need the owner's call.

- **Field-panel side on record pages.** Left (Pipedrive, Attio 2026, HubSpot classic) or right (HubSpot 2026, Salesforce)? Both are current. The recommendation is left, but if the current pages already put it right and users are used to that, consistency wins.
- **Autosave scope.** Which fields save on blur (status, owner, dates, tags) and which need an explicit Save (address, money, anything that triggers homeowner messages or automations)?
- **Dark mode.** We ship it, but no roofing competitor does. Keep it as a per-user Light/Dark/System option and commit to full coverage, or reduce the surface it must cover?
- **Sidebar chrome colour.** Ours is a charcoal rail (`--chrome` #363a43). The 2025–26 direction is a neutral, light shell, and HubSpot's dark-magenta nav drew mixed reactions. No source says dark sidebars are disliked. Keep or lighten?
- **Collapsed sidebar:** a 64px icon rail (Common) or keep fully hidden (Atlassian 2025)?
- **Info colour on Ember.** Add a dedicated blue `--info`, or accept orange-as-info and live with the risk of confusing it with warning and danger?
- **Offline scope.** Is the photo/note queue plus today's cached appointments enough, or do agents need offline estimate and signature capture (Leap does it; Jobber reportedly cannot)?
- **⌘K.** Keep it as a power-user extra (recommended), extend it to actions, or reduce it?
- **Insurance-claim fields.** What share of jobs are insurance? That decides between a conditional field group and AccuLynx-style supplement, permit and mortgage-check trackers.
- **Pipeline shape.** One pipeline spanning sales and production (Roofr, AccuLynx), or separate sales and production boards (JobNimbus-style)?
- **Stage drag rules.** Free drag, or gate some stages (Sold needs a signed estimate)?
- **Density.** Is a per-user compact toggle worth building, or are fixed 40px rows (48px on touch) enough?
- **Map view and financing.** Needed now, or left to canvassing tools and lender links?
- **Homeowner portal scope.** Single-document token links (quote, invoice) only, or a full per-job portal with photos, schedule and messages (AccuLynx, Jobber Client Hub)?
